The problem

The best data in youth football is locked behind a big budget.

GPS vests, multi-camera rigs and video analysts are standard at top academies. For everyone else they are completely out of reach.

At a top academy

  • GPS vests on every player
  • Multi-camera rigs around the pitch
  • Video analysts breaking down every match

Everywhere else

  • A phone propped on a tripod, or held by a parent
  • A recording that is watched once, then forgotten
  • No way to tell if you are really improving

A talented 15-year-old should not need a club budget to understand their own game. AcademyTracker is built for that player.

How it works

Three steps from final whistle to feedback.

Add your match

Upload the recording you already have, filmed on any phone. Add the opponent and the date, and you are ready to go.

Tag what happens while you watch

Watch the match in the app and tap a button each time something happens. The app saves what it was and the exact second of video.

PassShotTackleDribbleTurnover

Get statistics that link back to the footage

Your statistics, pitch maps and training plan are built from your tags. Tap any number to see the moments behind it and check it yourself.

What you get

Analysis you can trust, because you can check it.

Statistics linked to footage

Every number comes from moments you tagged, and every moment opens at the exact second it happened. No mystery figures — just evidence you can watch.

Pitch maps

See where your passes, shots and turnovers happened, so you can spot patterns in your game instead of guessing at them.

Season trends

One match is noise; a season is a signal. Track how you are really changing across the games you add.

A training plan that fits you

Get drills aimed at your weakest areas, with more work where you need it and less where you are already strong.
